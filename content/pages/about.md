# ./about

Hi, I'm Enzo. 👋

I'm a developer focused on **low-level systems and tooling**.

I enjoy building things, understanding how they work under the 
hood, and exploring the boundaries between software and systems. 
I'm particularly interested in **low-level programming languages
(Rust, C and Zig), Unix systems and Unix Philosophy, compilers,
open source, distributed systems and Web3**.

## What I do

My work and projects mainly cover systems-oriented areas, but I 
also create software for AI and cybersecurity.

I enjoy to work mostly close to the metal, but I work with 
high-level abstractions — whether that means designing a system, 
writing software, or simply taking something apart to understand 
how it works.

## This site

This is my little corner of the web.

Here you'll find my projects, technical writing, experiments, 
ideas, and things I've learned along the way.

Everything here is built with a preference for **simplicity, 
openness, and understanding how things work**.

## Open Source

Whenever possible, I build and share my work as open source.
