**Last updated: August 29, 2026**

This site is static: it doesn't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics, 
and it does not directly collect personal data except for information you 
voluntarily provide through the contact form, which is processed by Formspree.

See [Formspree's privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/)
for details on how that data is handled.

This site is hosted by Netlify. Netlify may process technical information 
associated with visitors' requests, such as IP addresses, browser information, 
and server logs, as part of providing, securing, and operating its hosting 
infrastructure.

See [Netlify's Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) 
for details on how Netlify handles personal data.

The source code of this website is publicly available in its
